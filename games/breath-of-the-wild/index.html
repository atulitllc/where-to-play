History

Climbing, cooking, and a Hyrule you can cross without a set dungeon order.

It launched on Wii U and Nintendo Switch in 2017. This one page names both. Tears of the Kingdom is the sequel and has its own page.

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild is recorded here as a 2017 game. The lead system is Nintendo Switch, 9th generation, hybrid console. Nintendo Switch is the hybrid console, played on a television or as a handheld. Current official listings, when they exist, are Nintendo's own. Every other Nintendo Switch game in this catalog is on the Nintendo Switch page.
