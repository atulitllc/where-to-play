History

A return to side-scrolling Mario on two screens, with a wall jump and a huge mushroom.

It restarted 2D Mario on DS and led to the Wii, Wii U, and later Deluxe games.

New Super Mario Bros. is recorded here as a 2006 game. The lead system is Nintendo DS, 7th generation, dual-screen handheld. Nintendo DS is the dual-screen handheld. Touch and the second screen are part of how many of these games are built. Every other Nintendo DS game in this catalog is on the Nintendo DS page.

This is one page for the same game on more than one Nintendo system. Also named here: Wii U. A remake, or a release that is a different work, gets its own page. The year goes in the name when two slugs would otherwise collide.

The credit chip on this page reads Nintendo. RAWG lists no developer name and no publisher name. If those names disagree with the chip, both stay on the page so the record can be checked.

RAWG's genre labels for this record are Platformer, Arcade, Action. They are a filing aid, not a review written for this page, and not a description of how the game feels in your hands.

The date field is 2006-05-15; the user rating is 4.17 out of 5 from 261 ratings. Those fields belong to RAWG. This page does not add a score of its own.

Series context: this title belongs with Mario. Other Mario pages in the catalog run from 1983 to 2006, including Mario Bros., Super Mario Bros., Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels, Super Mario Bros. 2, Super Mario Bros. 3. 16 more Mario pages exist here beyond the list.
