History

Gamebreaker tricks replace the season sim in FIFA Street, EA Sports BIG's 2005 street-soccer spinoff. Small courts, walls, and a trick meter are the sport, and the GameCube was part of that original multiplatform release.

The annual FIFA game is a different product. Here a goal is often the end of a juggle, and the roster is there to style on a playground rather than to hold a formation.

BIG's street spinoff is this disc. The cube release sits in the first wave, not a later numbered street sequel.
