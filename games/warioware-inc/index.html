History

Seconds-long microgames pile up until the speed is the joke.

It is the first WarioWare, the Game Boy Advance original of a series that spread to every later Nintendo handheld and home console.

WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$! is recorded here as a 2003 game. The lead system is Game Boy Advance, 6th generation, 32-bit handheld. Game Boy Advance is the widescreen handheld of the early 2000s, still on cartridges, with a library that sits between SNES-style games and later dual-screen designs. Every other Game Boy Advance game in this catalog is on the Game Boy Advance page.
