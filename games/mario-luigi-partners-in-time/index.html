History

Adult brothers team with their baby selves across two screens.

It is the DS follow-up to Superstar Saga.

Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time is recorded here as a 2005 game. The lead system is Nintendo DS, 7th generation, dual-screen handheld. Nintendo DS is the dual-screen handheld.
