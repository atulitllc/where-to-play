History

A theme-park of attractions that show off the GamePad, from a ghost house to a Luigi mansion minigame.

It is the Wii U launch compilation, a pack-in for many deluxe sets.

Nintendo Land is recorded here as a 2012 game. The lead system is Wii U, 8th generation, HD home console. Wii U is the HD home console before Switch, with a tablet-style GamePad. Its own digital catalog is not treated here as a current way to get a game. Every other Wii U game in this catalog is on the Wii U page.

This slug names Wii U only. RAWG sometimes tags the same record on other hardware. Those tags are not copied here unless this catalog treats them as the same Nintendo release.

The credit chip on this page reads Nintendo. RAWG lists developer Nintendo and publisher Nintendo. If those names disagree with the chip, both stay on the page so the record can be checked.
