History

A tiny robot cleans a house, earns happy points, and learns the family's problems at appliance scale.

It is the GameCube original of a small Nintendo-published series.

Chibi-Robo! is recorded here as a 2005 game. The lead system is GameCube, 6th generation, disc home console. GameCube is Nintendo's disc home console of the sixth generation. This page records the disc release, not a later reissue. Every other GameCube game in this catalog is on the GameCube page.
