History

Motion-controlled spells carry the living-room build of EA's film tie-in Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince, with a separate handheld game for the sixth movie. Quidditch and potion class break up the corridor sneaking Dumbledore assigns.

Hogwarts corridors, Quidditch, and potion class supply the activities between story scenes. The remote stands in for a wand, so a flick is the cast rather than a button assigned to a spell name.

The 2009 pair arrived with the movie, and the pocket version does not reuse the motion stages. Horcrux lessons from Dumbledore still frame why Harry is sneaking through those halls.
