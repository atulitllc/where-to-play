Final Fantasy

  • 1987
  • NES and Game Boy Advance and Wii
  • Square
  • 3rd generation

History

A party of four jobs crosses a cursed world in menu-driven battles.

It is the NES role-playing game that opened Square's Final Fantasy line.

Final Fantasy is recorded here as a 1987 game. The lead system is NES, 3rd generation, 8-bit home console. The NES is Nintendo's 8-bit home console, the Famicom in Japan. Games shipped on cartridges, and the library set the pattern for side-scrolling platformers. Every other NES game in this catalog is on the NES page.

This is one page for the same game on more than one Nintendo system. Also named here: Game Boy Advance and Wii. A remake, or a release that is a different work, gets its own page. The year goes in the name when two slugs would otherwise collide.

The credit chip on this page reads Square. RAWG lists no developer name and no publisher name. If those names disagree with the chip, both stay on the page so the record can be checked.

RAWG's genre labels for this record are Casual, RPG. They are a filing aid, not a review written for this page, and not a description of how the game feels in your hands.

The date field is 2002-10-05; the user rating is 3.79 out of 5 from 105 ratings; the ESRB label on the card is Everyone 10+. Those fields belong to RAWG. This page does not add a score of its own.

Series context: this title belongs with Final Fantasy. Other Final Fantasy pages in the catalog run from 1987 to 2010, including Final Fantasy (1987), Final Fantasy II (1988), The Final Fantasy Legend, Final Fantasy III (1990), Final Fantasy Legend II.

In this catalog, Final Fantasy is the family name. Same franchise lists spin-offs filed under their own heading, plus any further entries that did not fit the series list.
