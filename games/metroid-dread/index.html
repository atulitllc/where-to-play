History

A side-view Metroid about slipping past threats you cannot beat yet, then coming back armed.

It is a Switch original and the numbered follow-up to Fusion, developed with MercurySteam.

Metroid Dread is recorded here as a 2021 game. The lead system is Nintendo Switch, 9th generation, hybrid console. Nintendo Switch is the hybrid console, played on a television or as a handheld. Current official listings, when they exist, are Nintendo's own. Every other Nintendo Switch game in this catalog is on the Nintendo Switch page.
