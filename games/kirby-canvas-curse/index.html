History

You draw paths for a ball of Kirby instead of moving him directly.

It is the DS Kirby built entirely around the touch screen.

Kirby: Canvas Curse is recorded here as a 2005 game. The lead system is Nintendo DS, 7th generation, dual-screen handheld. Nintendo DS is the dual-screen handheld.
