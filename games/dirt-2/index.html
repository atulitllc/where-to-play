  • 2009
  • Wii and Nintendo DS
  • See the RAWG record

History

Festival weekends replace a lone rally season, and Colin McRae: DiRT 2 is Codemasters' off-road event with reduced living-room and handheld editions. Loose gravel still slides the car even in the smaller editions that lack the full damage model.

The high-definition game piles trucks, rallycross, and guest drivers into a touring series. Those Nintendo versions are not that full simulation; they are smaller takes on the same dusty brand.

The 2009 split matters when someone expects the big damage model on a remote. What remains is still a course of loose surfaces rather than a closed asphalt grand prix.
