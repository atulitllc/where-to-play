History

Crops and livestock fill the days in Harvest Moon 3 GBC (2000), the third color-handheld entry after Harvest Moon GB and Harvest Moon 2 GBC. A town remembers how the farmer treats it, and the daylight still runs out too soon.

Watering, shipping produce, and brushing an animal are the morning. Festivals and courtship fill the evening, with a bachelor or bachelorette whose heart meter moves when the farmer shows up with the right gift.

The series had already taught players that a farm is a calendar. This third pocket chapter adds another valley, another set of neighbors, and the same risk of letting the turnips die.
