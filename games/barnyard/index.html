History

Otis the cow walks upright through Barnyard, the 2006 tie-in to Nickelodeon's film about a farm of animals who misbehave when the farmer looks away. Chores, a race, and a party in the barn are the kinds of scenes the movie already staged.

The bullies are coyotes, and the farmer must not catch the animals standing. Minigames break up the yard exploration, which fits a film that was already a pile of gags rather than one long quest.

Nickelodeon's picture gave the cow a secret life. The game uses that secret, Otis's voice and his friends, as the excuse for the activities in the yard.
