Tetris Attack is recorded here as a 1995 game. The lead system is Game Boy, 4th generation, 8-bit handheld. Game Boy is the gray-brick handheld. Games are small cartridges meant for short sessions away from a television. Every other Game Boy game in this catalog is on the Game Boy page.
