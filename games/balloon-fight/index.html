History

Flapping balloons keep you aloft while you pop rivals and dodge lightning.

It is a small NES arcade-style game, often grouped with the console's early black-box lineup.

Balloon Fight is recorded here as a 1985 game. The lead system is NES, 3rd generation, 8-bit home console. The NES is Nintendo's 8-bit home console, the Famicom in Japan. Games shipped on cartridges, and the library set the pattern for side-scrolling platformers. Every other NES game in this catalog is on the NES page.
