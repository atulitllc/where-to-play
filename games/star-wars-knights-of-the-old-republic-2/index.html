History

Revan's past is the mystery in Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic, BioWare's Xbox and PC role-playing game set thousands of years before the films. Bastila Shan, Carth Onasi, and HK-47 board the Ebon Hawk, light and dark choices change the ending, and the voyage is the 2003 story rather than the later online game.

Dantooine, Tatooine, and the Star Forge are among the stops. Party banter shifts with those moral choices.

An online successor set in the same era reused the Old Republic name years afterward. This row is the single-player BioWare tale.
