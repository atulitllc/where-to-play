History

John Marston rides for the government in Red Dead Redemption, Rockstar San Diego's open-world Western, and later ports carried him to newer hardware. New Austin and Nuevo Paraíso hold Dutch's remaining gang, Dead Eye slows a gunfight, and the story arrived in 2010 from Rockstar San Diego.

Honor shifts when strangers are helped or robbed. Beecher's Hope is the ranch the ending refuses to treat lightly.

Nintendo Switch is one of those later ports. A prequel years afterward revisited the gang's earlier life, while this game stays Marston's assignment.
