Super Mario 3D World

  • 2013
  • Wii U and Nintendo Switch
  • Nintendo

History

Clear courses, a cat suit, and a pace closer to 2D Mario than to the sandbox 3D games.

This page covers the 2013 Wii U game and the later Switch edition, which adds Bowser's Fury as an extra story rather than a replacement.

Super Mario 3D World is recorded here as a 2013 game. The lead system is Wii U, 8th generation, HD home console. Wii U is the HD home console before Switch, with a tablet-style GamePad. Its own digital catalog is not treated here as a current way to get a game. Every other Wii U game in this catalog is on the Wii U page.

This is one page for the same game on more than one Nintendo system. Also named here: Nintendo Switch. A remake, or a release that is a different work, gets its own page. The year goes in the name when two slugs would otherwise collide.

The credit chip on this page reads Nintendo. RAWG lists no developer name and no publisher name. If those names disagree with the chip, both stay on the page so the record can be checked.

RAWG's genre labels for this record are Platformer, Action. They are a filing aid, not a review written for this page, and not a description of how the game feels in your hands.

The date field is 2013-11-21; the user rating is 4.36 out of 5 from 236 ratings; the ESRB label on the card is Everyone. Those fields belong to RAWG. This page does not add a score of its own.

Series context: this title belongs with Mario. Other Mario pages in the catalog run from 1983 to 2013, including Mario Bros., Super Mario Bros., Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels, Super Mario Bros. 2, Super Mario Bros. 3. 16 more Mario pages exist here beyond the list.

In this catalog, Mario is the family name. Same franchise lists spin-offs filed under their own heading, plus any further entries that did not fit the series list.

RAWG's own platform list for this record is nintendo-switch, wii-u. This catalog keeps only the Nintendo systems named above. A personal computer, or another company's console, on that list is not a claim that this page covers that version.

Related games are ordinary links inside this catalog: same series, same franchise, the same Nintendo system, and the same developer when RAWG names one. They are for browsing. Nothing in those lists is for sale, and none of them is a copy of the game.
