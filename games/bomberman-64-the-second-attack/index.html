History

Hudson returned to adventure stages and bombs in Bomberman 64: The Second Attack, the 2000 Nintendo 64 sequel. In Japan it is known as a numbered follow-up to Bomberman 64, and the story shares space with the series' explosives.

Pommy, a companion, and a gravity belt change how a bomb is thrown across a 3D room. The maze party of the Super Nintendo multitap is not the center; an exploration map is.

The Second Attack name is the Western label for that numbered follow-up. Adventure rooms, the bombs, and the companion are the sequel's mix.
