The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks

  • 2009
  • Nintendo DS and Wii U
  • Nintendo
  • 7th generation

History

A train replaces the boat, and Zelda is a spirit partner rather than a distant goal.

It is the second DS Zelda in the Wind Waker's art style, and a different adventure from Phantom Hourglass.

The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks is recorded here as a 2009 game. The lead system is Nintendo DS, 7th generation, dual-screen handheld. Nintendo DS is the dual-screen handheld. Touch and the second screen are part of how many of these games are built. Every other Nintendo DS game in this catalog is on the Nintendo DS page.

This is one page for the same game on more than one Nintendo system. Also named here: Wii U. A remake, or a release that is a different work, gets its own page. The year goes in the name when two slugs would otherwise collide.

The credit chip on this page reads Nintendo. RAWG lists no developer name and no publisher name. If those names disagree with the chip, both stay on the page so the record can be checked.

RAWG's genre labels for this record are Adventure, Action. They are a filing aid, not a review written for this page, and not a description of how the game feels in your hands.

The date field is 2009-12-07; the user rating is 3.91 out of 5 from 161 ratings; the ESRB label on the card is Everyone 10+. Those fields belong to RAWG. This page does not add a score of its own.
