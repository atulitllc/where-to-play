Metroid Prime

  • 2002
  • GameCube and Wii
  • Retro Studios / Nintendo
  • 6th generation

History

First-person exploration of a ruined planet, with a scan visor and suits that reopen old rooms.

It is the GameCube original. Metroid Prime Remastered on Switch is a separate page, not a replacement for this one.

Metroid Prime is recorded here as a 2002 game. The lead system is GameCube, 6th generation, disc home console. GameCube is Nintendo's disc home console of the sixth generation. This page records the disc release, not a later reissue. Every other GameCube game in this catalog is on the GameCube page.

This is one page for the same game on more than one Nintendo system. Also named here: Wii. A remake, or a release that is a different work, gets its own page. The year goes in the name when two slugs would otherwise collide.

The credit chip on this page reads Retro Studios / Nintendo. RAWG lists no developer name and no publisher name. If those names disagree with the chip, both stay on the page so the record can be checked.

RAWG's genre labels for this record are Shooter, Adventure, Action. They are a filing aid, not a review written for this page, and not a description of how the game feels in your hands.

The date field is 2002-11-17; the user rating is 4.35 out of 5 from 261 ratings. Those fields belong to RAWG. This page does not add a score of its own.

Series context: this title belongs with Metroid. Other Metroid pages in the catalog run from 1986 to 2021, including Metroid, Metroid II: Return of Samus, Super Metroid, Metroid Fusion, Metroid Prime 2: Echoes.

In this catalog, Metroid is the family name. Same franchise lists spin-offs filed under their own heading, plus any further entries that did not fit the series list.

RAWG's own platform list for this record is wii, gamecube. This catalog keeps only the Nintendo systems named above. A personal computer, or another company's console, on that list is not a claim that this page covers that version.
