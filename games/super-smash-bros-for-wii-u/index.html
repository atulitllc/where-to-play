History

The home-console half of the pair that also shipped on 3DS, with larger stages.

It is the Wii U Smash. The 3DS version is a separate page, and Ultimate on Switch is a later game.

Super Smash Bros. for Wii U is recorded here as a 2014 game. The lead system is Wii U, 8th generation, HD home console. Wii U is the HD home console before Switch, with a tablet-style GamePad. Its own digital catalog is not treated here as a current way to get a game. Every other Wii U game in this catalog is on the Wii U page.

This slug names Wii U only. RAWG sometimes tags the same record on other hardware. Those tags are not copied here unless this catalog treats them as the same Nintendo release.

The credit chip on this page reads Bandai Namco / Sora Ltd. / Nintendo. RAWG lists no developer name and no publisher name. If those names disagree with the chip, both stay on the page so the record can be checked.

RAWG does not list genres on this record. The page does not invent any.

Series context: this title belongs with Super Smash Bros.. Other Super Smash Bros. pages in the catalog run from 1999 to 2014, including Super Smash Bros., Super Smash Bros. (1999), Super Smash Bros. Melee, Super Smash Bros. Brawl, Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS.
