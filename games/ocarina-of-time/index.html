The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time

  • 1998
  • Nintendo 64
  • Nintendo

History

A rideable Hyrule and dungeons built around a small set of tools, with a lock-on camera for fights.

It is the Nintendo 64 original, not the later 3DS revision, which has its own page.

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is recorded here as a 1998 game. The lead system is Nintendo 64, 5th generation, 3D home console. Nintendo 64 is the first 3D home console in this catalog. Games used cartridges, and a lot of the library is built around analog-stick movement. Every other Nintendo 64 game in this catalog is on the Nintendo 64 page.

This slug names Nintendo 64 only. RAWG sometimes tags the same record on other hardware. Those tags are not copied here unless this catalog treats them as the same Nintendo release.

The credit chip on this page reads Nintendo. RAWG lists no developer name and no publisher name. If those names disagree with the chip, both stay on the page so the record can be checked.

RAWG's genre labels for this record are Adventure, Action, RPG. They are a filing aid, not a review written for this page, and not a description of how the game feels in your hands.

The date field is 1998-11-21; the user rating is 4.37 out of 5 from 892 ratings; the ESRB label on the card is Everyone. Those fields belong to RAWG. This page does not add a score of its own.

Series context: this title belongs with The Legend of Zelda. Other The Legend of Zelda pages in the catalog run from 1986 to 2013, including The Legend of Zelda, Zelda II: The Adventure of Link, The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past, The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (1993), The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask. 6 more The Legend of Zelda pages exist here beyond the list.

In this catalog, The Legend of Zelda is the family name. Same franchise lists spin-offs filed under their own heading, plus any further entries that did not fit the series list.

RAWG's own platform list for this record is nintendo-switch, nintendo-64. This catalog keeps only the Nintendo systems named above. A personal computer, or another company's console, on that list is not a claim that this page covers that version.
