History

George the gorilla, Lizzie the lizard, and Ralph the wolf smash buildings in Rampage, Bally Midway's arcade destruction. Eating a bystander and punching a skyscraper are both scoring methods, and the army eventually arrives to stop the movie monsters.

The coin-op dates to 1986, and three monsters can ruin a city block at once. George, Lizzie, and Ralph punching the same skyline is the cabinet's party trick.

The NES cart is a port of that arcade destruction. George, Lizzie, and Ralph are the same three monsters, brought home without becoming a new species.
