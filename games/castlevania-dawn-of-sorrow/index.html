History

Soma returns, and magic seals ask you to draw on the touch screen to finish a boss.

It is the DS sequel to Aria of Sorrow.

Castlevania: Dawn of Sorrow is recorded here as a 2005 game. The lead system is Nintendo DS, 7th generation, dual-screen handheld. Nintendo DS is the dual-screen handheld. Touch and the second screen are part of how many of these games are built. Every other Nintendo DS game in this catalog is on the Nintendo DS page.

This slug names Nintendo DS only. RAWG sometimes tags the same record on other hardware. Those tags are not copied here unless this catalog treats them as the same Nintendo release.
