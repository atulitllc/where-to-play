  • See the RAWG record

History

Coaster blueprints and midway minigames share Frontier's Thrillville: Off the Rails. The 2007 theme-park sequel includes a DS version.

Track pieces build a ride, then a minigame or a coaster run tests whether the park guests stay happy. It follows an earlier Thrillville park rather than starting the series.

The DS version carries building and minigames in that sequel's season. Roller coasters remain the headline attraction.
