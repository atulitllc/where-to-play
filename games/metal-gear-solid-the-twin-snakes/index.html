Metal Gear Solid: The Twin Snakes is the 2004 Metal Gear stop on GameCube, filed by RAWG under Adventure and Action.

RAWG may show other hardware; this catalog keeps the GameCube Nintendo line. A remake or distinct re-release uses a separate slug when it is a different work.

Metal Gear Solid: The Twin Snakes is recorded here as a 2004 game. The lead system is GameCube, 6th generation, disc home console. GameCube is Nintendo's disc home console of the sixth generation. This page records the disc release, not a later reissue. Every other GameCube game in this catalog is on the GameCube page.

This slug names GameCube only. RAWG sometimes tags the same record on other hardware. Those tags are not copied here unless this catalog treats them as the same Nintendo release.
