Super Star Wars

  • 1992
  • SNES
  • See the RAWG record
  • 16-bit home console

History

LucasArts and Sculptured Software's Super Star Wars adapts A New Hope as a 1992 Super Nintendo action game, Mode 7 races included. It opened a trilogy of film tie-ins on that console.
