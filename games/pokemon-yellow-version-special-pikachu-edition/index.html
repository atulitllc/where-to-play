Pokémon Yellow Version: Special Pikachu Edition

  • 1998
  • Game Boy and Game Boy Color
  • See the RAWG record
  • 4th generation

History

Game Freak's Pokémon Yellow Version: Special Pikachu Edition keeps Pikachu outside the ball and follows the anime more closely than Red and Green. Japan received this special edition of the first generation in 1998, and it stays inside Kanto.

Pikachu walks behind the trainer, and Jessie and James appear in scenes the earlier pair staged differently. Gyms still run from Pewter City toward the Indigo Plateau.

Red and Green, and the international Red and Blue pair, are the originals. Yellow dresses that same Kanto journey in the anime's Pikachu and a handful of altered encounters.
