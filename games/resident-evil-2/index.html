  • 1998
  • Nintendo 64 and GameCube

History

Leon Kennedy and Claire Redfield cross a ruined Raccoon City in Resident Evil 2 (1998) already. Capcom's PlayStation release splits the outbreak between the rookie cop and Chris Redfield's sister, and their routes meet at the police department.

Zombies, a licker, and the station's locked doors shape a hunt for keys and ammunition. Ink ribbons record a save on the original discs, and each character sees rooms the other misses.

The Nintendo 64 port is famous for fitting that two-disc game onto one cartridge. A GameCube release followed later, still the same Leon-and-Claire outbreak rather than a new city.
