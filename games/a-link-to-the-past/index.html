The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past

  • 1991
  • SNES and Game Boy Advance and Wii U and Nintendo 3DS and Wii
  • Nintendo
  • 4th generation

History

Two layered versions of the same kingdom, and dungeons that teach a tool and then test it.

It is the SNES Zelda that later top-down games, including the 2013 handheld one, openly echo.

The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past is recorded here as a 1991 game. The lead system is SNES, 4th generation, 16-bit home console. The Super Nintendo is the 16-bit home console that followed, with a deeper color range and a library of platformers, racers, and role-playing games. Every other SNES game in this catalog is on the SNES page.

This is one page for the same game on more than one Nintendo system. Also named here: Game Boy Advance and Wii U and Nintendo 3DS and Wii. A remake, or a release that is a different work, gets its own page. The year goes in the name when two slugs would otherwise collide.

The credit chip on this page reads Nintendo. RAWG lists no developer name and no publisher name. If those names disagree with the chip, both stay on the page so the record can be checked.

RAWG's genre labels for this record are Adventure, Action. They are a filing aid, not a review written for this page, and not a description of how the game feels in your hands.

The date field is 1991-11-21; the user rating is 4.35 out of 5 from 582 ratings; the ESRB label on the card is Everyone. Those fields belong to RAWG. This page does not add a score of its own.

Series context: this title belongs with The Legend of Zelda.
