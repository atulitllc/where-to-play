RUSH is recorded here as a 2010 game. The lead system is Wii U, 8th generation, HD home console. Wii U is the HD home console before Switch, with a tablet-style GamePad. Its own digital catalog is not treated here as a current way to get a game. Every other Wii U game in this catalog is on the Wii U page.

This slug names Wii U only. RAWG sometimes tags the same record on other hardware. Those tags are not copied here unless this catalog treats them as the same Nintendo release.
