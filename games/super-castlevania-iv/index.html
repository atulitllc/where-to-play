Super Castlevania IV

  • SNES and Nintendo 3DS

History

A whip that swings in more directions and a castle redrawn for 16-bit hardware.

It is the SNES Castlevania, a retelling rather than a straight port of the NES game.

Super Castlevania IV is recorded here as a 1991 game. The lead system is SNES, 4th generation, 16-bit home console. The Super Nintendo is the 16-bit home console that followed, with a deeper color range and a library of platformers, racers, and role-playing games. Every other SNES game in this catalog is on the SNES page.

This is one page for the same game on more than one Nintendo system. Also named here: Nintendo 3DS. A remake, or a release that is a different work, gets its own page. The year goes in the name when two slugs would otherwise collide.

The credit chip on this page reads Konami. RAWG lists no developer name and no publisher name. If those names disagree with the chip, both stay on the page so the record can be checked.

RAWG's genre labels for this record are Platformer, Adventure, Action. They are a filing aid, not a review written for this page, and not a description of how the game feels in your hands.

The date field is 1991-10-31; the user rating is 4.15 out of 5 from 99 ratings; the ESRB label on the card is Everyone 10+. Those fields belong to RAWG. This page does not add a score of its own.

Series context: this title belongs with Castlevania. Other Castlevania pages in the catalog run from 1986 to 2013, including Castlevania, Castlevania II: Simon's Quest (1987), Castlevania III: Dracula's Curse, Castlevania: The Adventure (1989), Castlevania II: Belmont's Revenge.

In this catalog, Castlevania is the family name. Same franchise lists spin-offs filed under their own heading, plus any further entries that did not fit the series list.

RAWG's own platform list for this record is pc, playstation4, xbox-one, nintendo-switch, nintendo-3ds, wii-u, wii, snes. This catalog keeps only the Nintendo systems named above. A personal computer, or another company's console, on that list is not a claim that this page covers that version.
