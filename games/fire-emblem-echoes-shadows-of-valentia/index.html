History

Alm and Celica lead separate armies in Fire Emblem Echoes: Shadows of Valentia, Intelligent Systems' remake of the Famicom game Gaiden. Villages open in a close third-person view, a dungeon crawl sits under the tactics, and the remake arrived in 2017 on the handheld.

The two-lord split stays from the nineteen-ninety-two original. Voice work and painted scenes are the new presentation on Nintendo 3DS.

Mila and Duma are the opposing gods. The remake keeps Gaiden's twin routes rather than copying Awakening's marriage focus.
