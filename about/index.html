About

A shelf, not a console.

Where to Play lists Nintendo games and the legal ways to play them: Nintendo Switch Online, official re-releases, and the system a game launched on. It is a browse catalog. We don't host ROMs or emulators. There is no play-in-browser.

Cover images come from RAWG. Every page that shows one names RAWG and links back. We did not pull artwork from Nintendo's galleries or from retailers.

Routes can go stale. When a page says Nintendo Switch Online or an official re-release, confirm it on Nintendo's website. If we are not sure a title is in a current library, the page says so instead of inventing a link.

Nothing here is for sale. Where to Play does not take orders and does not host games.
